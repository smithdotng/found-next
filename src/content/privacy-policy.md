Found Projects & Realty Limited ("Found", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. ## 1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

- Information you provide: name, email address, phone number, password, profile details, company information (for realtors and agents), and any content you submit such as property listings, inquiries, and messages.
- Transaction information: details relating to inquiries, bookings, and transactions facilitated through the platform.
- Automatically collected information: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and usage data collected through cookies and similar technologies.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Create and manage your account and provide our services;
- Display property listings and connect seekers with realtors and agents;
- Process and facilitate inquiries, bookings, and transactions;
- Communicate with you about your account, inquiries, and updates;
- Send you service notices and, where you have opted in, newsletters and marketing;
- Detect, prevent, and address fraud, security issues, and abuse;
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use.

## 3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it:

- With realtors, agents, or property owners when you submit an inquiry, so they can respond to you;

- When required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of Found, our users, or the public;
- In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

## 4. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, analyse traffic, and improve our services. You can control cookies through your browser settings, although some features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.
